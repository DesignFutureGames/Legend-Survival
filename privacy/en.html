2. Information Collected and Processed

The Developer does not operate its own user registration system or user database, and the Service is not designed to continuously store users' personal information on the Developer's own servers. However, the following information may be processed while using the Service.

  • Locally Stored Information: Game progress, settings, temporary data, and data stored on the user's device.
  • When Using Google Play Games Login: Player identification information, login status, achievements, leaderboard records, and information related to gameplay activity.
  • When Using Google Play Billing: Purchase history, purchase status, and transaction-related information necessary to verify and restore purchases.
  • When Using Google AdMob: Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, user interaction information, diagnostic information, and other data necessary for advertising, advertisement performance measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • When Using Firebase Analytics: App usage statistics, event records, usage patterns, device and application environment information, and identifiers used for analytics.
  • When Using Firebase Crashlytics: App crash information, error logs, device status at the time of a crash, operating system information, app version, and diagnostic information necessary for stability analysis.

Much of the information described above may be processed automatically through external services and SDKs, including Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics, rather than being collected directly by the Developer.

3. Purposes of Information Processing

The Developer may process information for the following purposes necessary to provide and operate the Service.

  • Providing essential game functions, including game execution, saving settings, and maintaining game progress.
  • Providing Google Play Games login, achievements, and leaderboard features.
  • Processing in-app purchases and verifying purchase restoration through Google Play Billing.
  • Displaying advertisements, measuring advertising performance, and preventing advertising fraud.
  • Analyzing usage patterns, improving content and features, and optimizing Service operations.
  • Analyzing and addressing app crashes, errors, unexpected shutdowns, and performance issues.
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent use, abuse, and abnormal activities.

4. Data Retention and Usage Period

Since the Developer does not operate its own server-based user database, the Developer generally does not separately retain personal information for extended periods.

  • Locally Stored Data: Data may be removed when the app is uninstalled, app data is cleared, the device is changed, or the user manually deletes the data.
  • Information Related to Google Play Games / Google Play Billing / Google AdMob / Firebase Services: Retention periods may vary depending on each service provider's policies, technical infrastructure, account status, and applicable laws.
  • Customer Support Emails: Emails may be retained for a reasonable period necessary to respond to user inquiries and resolve disputes.
